Why a clip maker

Hours of footage, a handful of moments worth posting

A long GTA 6 session or stream is mostly driving, menus and downtime, with a few moments that are worth a short: a chase that goes wrong, a shootout, a line that makes the whole chat laugh. Finding them by scrubbing a timeline takes longer than playing did.

Reelio does that first pass for you. It does not know the game; it listens. What is said, how the voice and the audio change, and how a moment is set up and paid off decide what rises to the top. That is why it works on GTA 6, and just as well on any other game you record with commentary.

How it works

From a GTA 6 recording to shorts in three steps

  1. 01

    Add a free key, once

    Paste a Google Gemini or OpenRouter API key in Reelio's Settings. It is stored only in your browser. One key is enough.

  2. 02

    Drop your recording

    Choose the video file. Reelio transcribes it on your device, or reads the subtitles that came with the file, then finds candidate moments and ranks them.

  3. 03

    Trim, style, export

    Pick the clips you like, adjust the start and end, choose captions and layout, and download 9:16 MP4 files one by one or all at once.

What makes it different

Built to run on your machine

  • Runs locally, nothing uploaded

    Your video is decoded, transcribed and rendered inside your browser tab. Only transcript text and a few audio numbers go to the model provider you chose.

  • Free with your own key

    No account, no watermark, no per-clip pricing. Gemini and OpenRouter both have free keys; their limits apply.

  • Ranked clips, not a pile

    Each candidate is scored on event arc, hook, standalone context, reaction, novelty, pacing, audio clarity and discussion, and you can see the breakdown.

  • Captions built in

    Burned-in captions in three styles, timed to the words, with an on/off switch.

  • Facecam layout

    Finds your facecam and stacks it above the gameplay in the vertical frame. Move it, resize it or turn it off; or use centre crop, or fit with a blurred background.

  • Works on long streams

    The file is streamed rather than loaded whole, so multi-hour recordings are fine. Results are cached, so asking again does not redo the transcript.

  • Uses existing subtitles

    If the video has embedded text subtitles, or you drop a .srt or .vtt file with it, Reelio uses those and skips transcription.

  • Trim before you export

    Every clip has start and end handles, so you can tighten the moment before it is rendered.

Straight talk

What to know before you start

  • It needs the file

    Reelio works on a video file on your computer. It does not take Twitch or YouTube links and cannot record a live stream.

  • Talking helps

    The ranking leans on speech and loudness. Commentary, reactions and voice chat give it plenty to work with; silent gameplay gives it less.

  • It is a shortlist

    A language model plus audio measurements picks the moments. Review the clips; the order is a guide, not a guarantee of views.

  • Desktop browser

    Built for a recent desktop Chrome or Edge. WebGPU speeds up transcription a lot; without it, it still works, more slowly. Clips export at 720×1280.

  • You post them yourself

    Reelio gives you MP4 files. Uploading to TikTok, Shorts or Reels is up to you, and so is checking that you may post the footage.

Questions

GTA 6 clip maker FAQ

What is a GTA 6 clip maker?

A tool that finds the best moments in a long GTA 6 recording, whether it is your own gameplay or a stream you saved, and cuts them into short vertical clips. Reelio does this in your browser: it transcribes the audio, ranks the moments and renders 9:16 MP4 clips with optional captions and a facecam layout.

Is Reelio free to use for GTA 6 clips?

Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no per-clip fee. You bring your own API key from Google Gemini or OpenRouter, which both offer free keys with daily limits set by them.

Is my GTA 6 footage uploaded?

No. Decoding, transcription and rendering happen in your browser tab, so the video file never leaves your computer. Only the transcript text and a few numbers measured from the audio, such as loudness, are sent to the model provider whose key you entered. The speech model is downloaded once from Hugging Face and cached by your browser.

Can I clip a GTA 6 stream?

Yes, as long as you have the recording as a file, such as a stream you saved or a VOD you are allowed to download. Reelio does not take Twitch or YouTube links and does not capture live streams.

Does it work if there is no commentary?

Less well. Reelio ranks moments mostly from what is said and how loud the audio gets. Silent gameplay gives it only loudness to go on, so expect a rougher shortlist. Recordings with commentary, a streamer's reactions or voice chat work best.

Does it keep my facecam?

Reelio looks for a facecam automatically and can stack it above the gameplay in the vertical frame. Detection is not perfect, so you can adjust the box or switch it off. Without a facecam you can choose centre crop or fit with a blurred background.

Am I allowed to post GTA 6 clips?

Reelio does not decide that. It depends on whose footage it is and on the publisher's and the platform's rules. Rockstar Games publishes a policy for videos featuring its games, platforms such as YouTube have their own monetization rules, and clipping someone else's stream normally needs their permission. Check before you post.

What do I need to run it?

A recent desktop Chrome or Edge and a free Gemini or OpenRouter key. Processing time depends on the recording's length and your computer: a GPU with WebGPU is much faster than the fallback, and a file with subtitles skips transcription. Audio is held in memory while it is processed, roughly 230 MB per hour of recording.
